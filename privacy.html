Your privacy, kept simple.

Last updated 28 October 2026

What this site collects

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers on this site, and it sets no cookies.

Your browser remembers a few small things on your own device:

  • whether you chose sound on or off, and when you last visited, for up to 30 days, so the opening doesn’t repeat every time;
  • the currency you picked in the price calculator;
  • for this visit only, the site that sent you here (for example Instagram or Google). If you then book a free consultation call, that name is added to your booking, so I know how you found me.

None of this is sent to me while you browse. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

When you contact me

When you email me or book a free consultation call, I receive what you choose to send: your name, your email address, and what you tell me about your book. I use it only to reply to you, plan your book and do the work you ask for.

  • I never sell or share your details with anyone.
  • Your manuscript and files stay private and are used only for your book.
  • You can ask me at any time to see or delete what I hold about you.

Outside services

The site's fonts are stored on this site itself, so no font service sees your visit. Email goes through your own email provider and mine (Gmail). When you book a free consultation call, the booking page is run by Cal.com: it receives the name, email and answers you type there, adds the call to my Google Calendar and creates the call link. Cal.com and Google each follow their own privacy terms.
