How much does it cost to illustrate a children's book?

Updated 31 October 2026 · 2 min read · Jooneyd Raza

Short answer

Illustrating a full picture book typically costs from about $1,500 to $7,000, and Reedsy's 2026 guide puts a 24-page children's book at $1,890–$6,990. The price depends mostly on how many pieces you need, how detailed the backgrounds are, and whether the cover and print-ready layout are included.

What it costs with me, Jooneyd Raza: My picture books, with art, layout and cover, are $1,499 for 24 pages, $1,899 for 32 and $2,299 for 40. Single pages of art are $149 each.

Published rates

All figures are in US dollars. No neutral UK or EU survey was found.

  • Reedsy guide (13 Aug 2026): $1,890–$6,990 to hire a freelance illustrator for a 24-page children's book.
  • Reedsy illustration page: $60–$200 per single page and $100–$400 per two-page spread, so a 30-page interior comes to $1,500–$6,000.

Count the pieces before you ask for quotes

The number of illustrations drives the quote more than anything else. Map your 24 or 32 pages into full pages, two-page spreads and small spot art first. A spread is one piece of art across two pages, and it usually costs more than a single page.

Put the print specs in the contract

IngramSpark warns that a book built from low-resolution illustrations can't simply be fixed later. So agree the print specs in writing before the art starts.

  • 300 DPI at the final printed size.
  • 0.125 in of bleed past the trim on full-page art.
  • CMYK files for IngramSpark color interiors.
  • Who makes the cover, and whether layout and lettering are included.
