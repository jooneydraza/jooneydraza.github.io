How much is reasonable to spend on a book cover?

Updated 31 October 2026 · 2 min read · Jooneyd Raza

Short answer

A professionally designed book cover usually costs from a few hundred dollars to about $1,500, and Reedsy's analysis of more than 9,600 cover projects in 2025 puts the average at $880. The price rises with custom illustration, a full print wrap, extra formats such as hardcover, series branding and tight deadlines.

What it costs with me, Jooneyd Raza: My covers (ebook + paperback) start at $599. Each next cover in the same series is $450, and a hardcover adds $150.

What authors pay: published market data

Two Reedsy data sets give a clear picture. Read them side by side rather than blending them.

  • Reedsy cover-cost post (23 Dec 2025): average $880, with most projects between $625 and $1,250.
  • Reedsy self-publishing cost guide (updated 13 Aug 2026): median $930, with over half of projects between $630 and $1,200.

Genre and project type change the number

In Reedsy's December 2025 data, fantasy has the highest genre median at $910. Picture-book covers sit at $470, but those are cover-only projects; most picture-book covers are part of a full illustration contract.

The Editorial Freelancers Association's 2026 member survey shows a lower book-cover median of $350 (mean $431). The EFA is an editorial association, so its design sample is small.

What you're buying

Each extra version of the cover is extra work, so the list of formats shapes the quote. From simplest to most work:

  • An ebook front cover only (KDP's ideal size is 2,560 × 1,600 px).
  • A paperback wrap (back, spine and front) sized to your page count.
  • A hardcover case cover with a 0.51 in wrap.
  • A separate version built on IngramSpark's own cover template.

A note on currency

All figures above are in US dollars, from US-led marketplaces. No neutral survey of cover prices in pounds or euros was found.
