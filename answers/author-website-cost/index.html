How much does an author website cost?

Updated 31 October 2026 · 2 min read · Jooneyd Raza

Short answer

Building it yourself costs about $60–$500 a year for the domain, website builder and newsletter, according to Reedsy's 2026 guide. Hiring a web designer to build one from scratch averages around $1,740 on Reedsy, plus the same yearly running costs.

What it costs with me, Jooneyd Raza: My one-book site is $899, a 5-page author website $1,449, and a custom motion site of up to 8 pages $2,449.

Yearly running costs

These figures are from Reedsy's guide (Aug 2026), in US dollars a year.

  • Domain: $8–$12 (for example Namecheap or GoDaddy).
  • Website builder or hosting: $48–$300 (for example WordPress or Squarespace).
  • Email newsletter: $0–$300. Mailchimp is free up to 500 contacts.
  • Squarespace's yearly plans include the domain free for the first year on new sites.

Builder or designer?

A builder suits a first book. You pick a template and add your cover, blurb and store links yourself, so the main cost is your time plus the yearly fees.

A designer makes more sense when you have several books or a series, want the site to match your covers, or don't want to learn the tool. Reedsy puts the average designer-built author site at about $1,740.

What every author site needs

  • Each book, with links to every store that sells it.
  • An about page.
  • A newsletter sign-up.
  • A contact page.
  • Pages that load fast on phones.
  • A domain registered in your own name, so you keep it if you change designer.

My prices

A one-book site, one long page for your book and its launch, is $899. An author website with 5 pages (home, books, about, newsletter, contact) is $1,449. A custom motion site with up to 8 pages, custom design and motion is $2,449.
