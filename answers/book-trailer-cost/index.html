How much does a book trailer cost?

Updated 31 October 2026 · 2 min read · Jooneyd Raza

Short answer

A 2026 guide puts book trailers from about $100 for a slideshow to $10,000 or more for live action: $200–$1,500 for moving text and $500–$5,000 for 2D animation. Length, the number of sizes, animation or footage and the music licence decide where yours lands.

Published price ranges

There is no neutral survey of trailer prices. These ranges come from 2026 guides, not from market averages.

  • Slideshow (still images, text and music): $100–$800.
  • Kinetic typography (moving text): $200–$1,500, with two to four weeks' turnaround.
  • 2D animation: $500–$5,000. 3D animation often starts at $3,000.
  • Live action: $1,000 to $10,000 or more.
  • UK (in pounds): a full trailer at £300–£1,500, and a freelance editor at £50–£200 per short video.

What drives the price

  • Length: Amazon's Sponsored Brands video ads accept 6 to 45 seconds and recommend 20 seconds or less. A 30–45 second cut also works in social feeds.
  • Sizes: every extra shape is another edit. Amazon's video ads take 16:9 only, while Reels and TikTok are vertical 9:16.
  • Footage or animation: stills and moving text cost the least, animation and filmed scenes the most.
  • Music licence: the track must be cleared for commercial use. TikTok's and Instagram's built-in music libraries are for personal posts, not for promoting a product.
  • Extras: short teasers for the weeks before launch, or an animated cover for ads.

Is it worth the money?

A trailer earns its cost when you reuse it in ads and posts, not when you post it once at launch. Compare quotes on what you get: length, sizes and the music licence, not the headline price.
