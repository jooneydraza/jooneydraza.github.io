My story

A designer who publishes too.

I am a book designer and a self-published author. I publish my own books on Amazon KDP and other platforms, so I know their rules and where books get stuck, and you never have to learn the technical side yourself.

Over 7+ years I have designed 1,000+ books: first novels, family memoirs, poetry, faith books, workbooks and children’s picture books. Books I design can go live for readers in 180+ countries, and my authors’ books have earned 3.5M+ in royalties.
