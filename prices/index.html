Formatting and the whole book are priced by length, one price for every book inside a band: up to 120 pages: formatting $249, whole book $749; 121–350 pages: formatting $349, whole book $849; 351–550 pages: formatting $399, whole book $899; over 550 pages: formatting $449, whole book $949.
